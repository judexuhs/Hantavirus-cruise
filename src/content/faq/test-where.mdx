---
question: "Where can I get tested?"
order: 50
tags: ["passengers", "clinical"]
---

Hantavirus testing is **not** a routine point-of-care service. It is performed at reference
laboratories using serology (IgM/IgG ELISA) or RT-PCR on blood. If you have symptoms after a
plausible exposure, present to a clinician — emergency department if symptoms are severe — and
ask them to coordinate testing with your country's national reference lab. The clinician should
know your travel history, including the ship and dates.
