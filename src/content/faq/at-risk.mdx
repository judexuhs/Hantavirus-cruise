---
question: "Was I at risk if I sailed on the affected ship?"
order: 10
tags: ["passengers", "risk"]
---

If you sailed on the MV Hondius voyage that departed Ushuaia on **April 1, 2026**, you should
treat yourself as potentially exposed and monitor for symptoms for **at least 6 weeks** after
disembarking. The same applies to close household and travel contacts of anyone who sailed.

That does not mean infection is likely. As of the most recent WHO update, the attack rate is in
the single digits across roughly 150 people on board, and the index exposure is currently
believed to have happened *before* boarding (in Argentina), not on the ship itself — though
some onboard close-contact transmission cannot be ruled out. Watch the Timeline for each new
datapoint from ECDC, WHO, and Argentine health authorities.
